# Agency Agents — Marketing Specialist Suite

## Source

The **msitarzewski/agency-agents** repository — an open-source collection of **230+ specialist agents** organized across **18 divisions**, released under the **MIT license**. This is one of the most comprehensive agent frameworks available for digital marketing operations.

## Marketing & Paid Media Agents (15 Specialists)

### Organic / Content Agents

**SEO Specialist** — Operates across five core pillars: technical SEO, on-page optimization, content strategy, link building, and performance measurement. Includes a cannibalization audit workflow that identifies when multiple pages compete for the same keyword intent, then recommends consolidation or differentiation strategies.

**AEO Foundations** — Focused on Answer Engine Optimization. Produces structured outputs like `llms.txt` files and WebMCP configurations that make content machine-readable for AI-powered search engines and citation systems.

**AI Citation Strategist** — Runs a six-platform audit covering Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Bing Copilot, ChatGPT with browsing, You.com, and Claude. Maps which platforms cite the brand, with what context, and identifies gaps where competitors are winning AI citations.

**Content Creator** — Generates marketing copy, blog posts, social content, and long-form articles with built-in SEO alignment. Operates within the methodology framework defined in [[Digital-Marketing-Pro-Methodology]].

**Growth Hacker** — Experimental, data-driven agent focused on rapid-cycle testing: viral loops, referral mechanics, product-led growth tactics, and conversion rate experiments.

**Email Strategist** — Designs full email marketing programs: segmentation, drip sequences, subject line testing, deliverability optimization, and revenue attribution.

### Paid Media Agents

**PPC Strategist** — Plans and optimizes paid search campaigns across Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising. Handles campaign structure, bidding strategy, and budget allocation.

**Search Query Analyst** — Mines search query reports for negative keyword opportunities, new match types, and audience intent signals that inform both paid and organic strategy.

**Paid Media Auditor** — Systematic review of existing ad accounts: wasted spend detection, structural issues, missed extensions, and performance benchmarking against industry standards.

**Tracking Specialist** — Ensures measurement integrity across the stack: conversion tracking, UTM governance, server-side tagging, and attribution model configuration.

**Ad Creative Strategist** — Develops ad copy frameworks, tests messaging angles, and maintains creative performance libraries across search and social formats.

**Programmatic Buyer** — Manages display and programmatic campaigns: audience targeting, supply path optimization, frequency capping, and brand safety.

**Paid Social Strategist** — Plans paid social campaigns across Meta, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, TikTok, and emerging platforms. Covers audience building, creative formats, and platform-specific optimization.
