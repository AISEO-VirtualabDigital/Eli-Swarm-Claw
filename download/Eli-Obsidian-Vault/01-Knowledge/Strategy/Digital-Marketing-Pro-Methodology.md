# Digital Marketing Pro — Methodology

## Source

The **digital-marketing-pro** methodology is a structured, agent-driven framework for building digital marketing strategies. It defines a repeatable process that produces consistent, auditable, and actionable strategy outputs.

## 12-Part Strategy Flow (61 Steps)

The methodology progresses through twelve phases with 61 discrete steps total:

1. **Discovery & Intake** — Client brief analysis, business model mapping, stakeholder identification
2. **Market & Audience Research** — TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, persona development, competitive landscape
3. **Channel Audit** — Current performance across all active and potential channels
4. **Technical Assessment** — Website health, tracking infrastructure, data quality review
5. **Keyword & Intent Mapping** — Search demand analysis, intent classification, opportunity scoring
6. **Content Gap Analysis** — Existing content inventory vs. demand, coverage scoring
7. **Competitive Intelligence** — Share of voice, backlink profiles, content strategy benchmarking
8. **Strategy Formulation** — Channel prioritization, budget allocation, sequencing
9. **AEO/GEO Alignment** — Answer Engine and Generative Engine Optimization integration (see [[SEO-Architecture-Patterns]])
10. **Forecasting & Modeling** — Three-scenario projections (conservative, moderate, aggressive)
11. **Implementation Roadmap** — Phased execution plan with milestones and dependencies
12. **Measurement Framework** — KPI definition, reporting cadence, optimization loops

## Two-Views Model

Every strategic recommendation is produced in two passes:

- **View 1 (Unbiased):** Raw analysis based solely on data and best practices, before any client-specific context filters in. This prevents anchoring bias.
- **View 2 (Validated):** View 1 filtered through client constraints — budget, timeline, team capacity, brand risk tolerance, and historical performance. The deliverable view.

This separation ensures the methodology produces honest analysis even when client pressures might otherwise distort recommendations.

## Stone vs. Opinion Tagging

Every insight in the methodology is tagged as either:

- **Stone:** Fact-based, data-backed, independently verifiable. Keyword volume, current rankings, budget spent, conversion rates.
- **Opinion:** Expert judgment, heuristic-based, experience-informed. "This channel will likely outperform because..."

Clients and team members can instantly distinguish between what is known and what is interpreted.

## Decision Matrix

A scoring framework that weighs opportunities across: estimated impact, implementation effort, time to results, resource requirements, and risk level. Produces a prioritized shortlist from the full opportunity set.

The methodology is executed by **24 specialist agents** spanning organic, paid, content, technical, and analytics disciplines. See [[Agency-Agents-Marketing-Suite]] for the full agent catalog.

## Advanced Frameworks

- **AEO/GEO 6-Platform Audit:** Systematic assessment of brand visibility across Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Bing Copilot, ChatGPT, You.com, and Claude
- **Five Digital Markets:** Framework for categorizing clients by digital maturity and market position
- **Unit Economics:** CAC, LTV, payback period, and margin analysis tied to channel performance
- **B2B DMU:** Decision-Making Unit mapping for complex B2B sales cycles
- **Three-Scenario Forecasting:** Conservative, moderate, and aggressive projections with explicit assumptions documented for each
