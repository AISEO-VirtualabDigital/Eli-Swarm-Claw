   186→   685→        11. Generative Engine Optimization and AI Search Readiness
   187→   686→Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) represents a critical shift in search optimization, focusing on structuring information so large language models can retrieve, synthesize, and cite it within conversational search interfaces.
   188→   687→The theoretical framework for AI visibility models how content density and structure impact retrieval likelihood :
   189→   688→$$V_{ai}=\alpha\cdot S_{density}+\beta\cdot Q_{addition}+\gamma\cdot C_{sources}+\delta\cdot F_{fluency}$$
   190→   689→Where:
   191→   690→* $V_{ai}$ represents the aggregate AI Search visibility and citation probability score.
   192→   691→* $S_{density}$ represents the density of verifiable numeric statistics and entity-specific metrics.
   193→   692→* $Q_{addition}$ represents the integration of direct, attributed expert quotations.
   194→   693→* $C_{sources}$ represents the deployment of peer-reviewed or authoritative external source citations.
   195→   694→* $F_{fluency}$ represents the syntactic clarity, structural extractability, and readability level of the copy.
   196→   695→Additionally, the Local Search Prominence Model evaluates map pack visibility through localized signals :
   197→   696→$$P_{local}=w_{1}\cdot GBP_{opt}+w_{2}\cdot Cit_{unstruct}+w_{3}\cdot Rev_{score}-w_{4}\cdot D_{centroid}$$
   198→   697→Where:
   199→   698→* $P_{local}$ represents the local visibility score inside Map Packs and localized searches.
   200→   699→* $GBP_{opt}$ represents the optimization score of the Google Business Profile (primary categories, attributes).
   201→   700→* $Cit_{unstruct}$ is the volume and quality of unstructured local citations (press, neighborhood guides).
   202→   701→* $Rev_{score}$ represents the frequency, volume, and sentiment rating of local reviews.
   203→   702→* $D_{centroid}$ represents the physical distance from the searcher centroid to the business location.
