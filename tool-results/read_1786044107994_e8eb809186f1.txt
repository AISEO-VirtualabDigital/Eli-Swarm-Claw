   129→   328→   328→## The 12-Part Engagement Methodology
   130→   329→   329→
   131→   330→   330→| Part | Name | Output |
   132→   331→   331→|------|------|--------|
   133→   332→   332→| 1 | Client Inputs | Stone vs Opinion intake (what client knows for certain vs what they believe) |
   134→   333→   333→| 2 | External Research | Unbiased market research (no client docs used) |
   135→   334→   334→| 3 | **Four Core Documents** | 61 explicit steps — Business & SBU (18), Segmentation (15), Brand Positioning (19), DMFlow (9) |
   136→   335→   335→| 4 | Competitive + Customer + Market | 4 unbiased analysis documents (4.1–4.4) |
   137→   336→   336→| 5 | **Client Validation Document** | The one true stop — client accepts/rejects/edits each finding |
   138→   337→   337→| 6 | Selective v2 Re-runs | Subset of Part 3 + Part 4 docs re-run per the Decision Matrix |
   139→   338→   338→| 7 | Preparation Documents | Internal operating layer (campaign architecture, KPI tree, content pillars, asset inventory, approval chains) |
   140→   339→   339→| 8 | **Growth Plan + Yearly Planner** | The flagship 11-section client-facing strategy + 12-month operational calendar |
   141→   340→   340→| 9 | Channel Strategy Fan-out | Up to 17 channel docs grouped into 7 families |
   142→   341→   341→| 10 | Execution Artefacts | Ad copy, post copy, headlines, CTAs |
   143→   342→   342→| 11 | AI Creative Instructions | Visual asset briefs with C2PA + EU Article 50 clauses |
   144→   343→   343→| 12 | **Continuous Improvement Loop** | Quarterly briefs feeding signals back into product/offering decisions |
   145→   344→   344→
   146→   345→   345→**Key architectural concepts:**
   147→   346→   346→- **Two-Views Model** — Every engagement carries v1 (unbiased market view) and v2 (client-validated view) after Part 5. Operating decisions reference v2; ideation references both. v1 is never deleted.
   148→   347→   347→- **Stone vs Opinion** — Every fact captured at intake is tagged with confidence. Stone = client knows for certain. Opinion = client believes (becomes a research question, not ground truth).
   149→   348→   348→- **Decision Matrix** — Maps client validation responses to which v1 documents need v2 re-runs. Prevents over- and under-re-running.
   150→   349→   349→- **Update-Back Rule** — Live operations surface corrections → source documents get versioned (v2.1, v2.2 …) → Living Project Instruction File propagates the change to all downstream skills.
   151→   350→   350→- **Living Project Instruction File** — Single source of truth per engagement. All skills read it first.
   152→   351→   351→
   153→   352→   352→15+ strategic-framework reference documents in `skills/context-engine/` support the methodology (Five Digital Markets, Channel Families, In-Market vs Out-Market, Multi-Dimensional Decision Framework, Unit Economics, Actionable Persona Format, B2B Decision-Making Unit, Three-Scenario Forecasting, 30/60/90-Day Framework, Reporting Cadence, Fixed vs Variable Budget, Competitor 3-Question Output, India Market Context, and more).

   263→   462→   462→## How the SEO skills chain together
   264→   463→   463→
