   318→   318→   318→   318→* Problem Awareness: The business owner experiences a sudden drop in lead consistency or notices that local competitors are ranking higher on Google Maps. Their thoughts focus on immediate cash flow survival, creating search queries like "why am I not showing up on Google Maps" or "how to get local plumbing calls."   
   319→   319→   319→   319→* Research Phase: The owner researches alternatives to expensive paid ads and encounters terms like Local SEO, technical architecture, and Generative Engine Optimization. They actively seek plain-language, un-gated explanations that break down these mechanisms without forcing them to submit contact details.   
   320→   320→   320→   320→* Comparing Agencies: The buyer compares niche service providers, evaluating established brands like Hook Agency or Rival Digital against boutique fractional operators. They audit agency websites to verify if they publish real pricing ranges, own their designs, or rely on restrictive 12-month contract locks.

The final buying trigger is the delivery of a highly detailed, personalized website diagnostic audit within 48 hours.
