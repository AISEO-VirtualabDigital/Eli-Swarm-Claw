o communicate with your Stash (to create markers, for example, or to search your file system) _might_ need to be configured to talk to your local Stash: by default they will use `http://localhost:9999/graphql` with no authentication to make their queries, but if your setup requires otherwise then you can find `py_common/config.ini` and set your own values.
