## Failure Scenarios & Mitigation
### Lambda Function Failures
- **Problem**: Function timeout or error
- **Mitigation**: Dead letter queues, automatic retries, CloudWatch alarms
### DynamoDB Throttling
- **Problem**: Hot partition or burst capacity exceeded
