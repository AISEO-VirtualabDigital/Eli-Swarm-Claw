GoHighLevel disappears and your brand takes over. In the SaaS settings, upload your logo, set your brand colours, and configure your custom domain so clients log in at app.youragency.com instead of a HighLevel URL. To point your domain at GHL: In your DNS provider, create a CNAME record for the subdomain (e.g. app ). Point that CNAME at the target GoHighLevel gives you in the white-label settings.
