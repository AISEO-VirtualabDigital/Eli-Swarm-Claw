### Ad Creative Strategist

**Core Mission:** Develop ad creative that maximizes click-through and conversion rates through systematic testing and audience-aligned messaging.
**Framework:** Develop 3-5 creative concepts per campaign based on value proposition angles (benefit-driven, feature-driven, social proof, urgency, question-led).
