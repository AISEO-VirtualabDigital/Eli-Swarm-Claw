## Adaptico OS (adaptico/adaptico-os)

**What it does:** A go-to-market audit and scoring platform that evaluates businesses across seven dimensions: Market Fit, Channel Strategy, Content Quality, Technical Foundation, Competitive Positioning, Growth Readiness, and Operational Scalability. Each dimension is scored 0-100 with specific sub-metrics.
