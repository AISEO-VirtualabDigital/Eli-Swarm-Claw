### Privacy First
Your data stays private. YouMind never uses your clips, notes, or content to train AI models. Everything you save is securely stored and yours alone.
## Why YouMind over NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is excellent for analyzing uploaded documents, but YouMind goes further — capturing live web content, PDFs, YouTube videos, and podcasts.
