Redirects support [PCRE regex syntax](http://php.net/manual/en/reference.pcre.pattern.syntax.php). By default, all `/` and `?` not inside parenthesis are escaped. To prevent any escaping include the opening and closing forward slashes and flags: `/^blog$/i`.
