kill turns a messy keyword list into a page plan. Your agent sorts keywords, compares intent, and decides which terms belong on the same page.
**Source**: https://openseo.so/docs/skills/keyword-clustering
### What it does

- Group keywords by intent, SERP similarity, and page type
- Separate terms that look similar but need different pages
- Map clusters to existing URLs when they fit
