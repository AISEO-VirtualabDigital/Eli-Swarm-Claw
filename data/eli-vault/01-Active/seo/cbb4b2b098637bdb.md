he Check Rankability for SERP report analyzes the results page and the pages ranking on it, then tells you how to rank for that search query. It reads the SEO difficulty data and suggests the best approach to rank, along with a page title, description and URL. It also tells you the minimum Domain Authority, referring domains and backlinks you need to reach the first page for that query.
