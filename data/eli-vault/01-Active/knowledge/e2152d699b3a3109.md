and 2 a.m. outage. Instatic is the opposite bet. One Bun server holds the whole thing — the canvas editor, the content engine, media, auth, forms, plugins, and the publisher — and you run it wherever you like, backed by SQLite or Postgres.
What comes out the other end is the part most builders quietly compromise on: plain semantic HTML and compact CSS, with none of the editor's machinery left behind in the page.
