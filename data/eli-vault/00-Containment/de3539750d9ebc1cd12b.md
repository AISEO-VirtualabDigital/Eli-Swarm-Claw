### Using Proxies

For larger scraping jobs, proxies help avoid rate limiting. Here's how to configure them:
```bash
./google-maps-scraper \
  -input queries.txt \
  -results results.csv \
  -proxies 'socks5://user:pass@host:port,http://host2:port2' \
  -depth 1 -c 2
