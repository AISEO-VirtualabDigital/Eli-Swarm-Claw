### How does Claude SEO assess E-E-A-T?

E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) is evaluated against the Search Quality Rater Guidelines, last updated September 2025 with YMYL expanded to include political and social topics. Experience signals: original research, case studies, first-hand photos. Expertise: author credentials and topical depth.
