# Skill 009 — Memory Stack & Retain

See [[SKILL-009-Memory-Stack-Retain]] for full specification.

Eli must treat every absorbed piece of information as a persistent memory asset. This skill governs acquisition, storage, retrieval, and maintenance of the entire knowledge base. No human-supplied knowledge may be silently dropped. Every absorption batch must be logged.
