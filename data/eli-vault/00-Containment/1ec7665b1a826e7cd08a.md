## OpenSEO (every-app/open-seo)

**What it does:** A full-featured open-source SEO platform that provides keyword research, rank tracking, site auditing, backlink analysis, and competitor monitoring. It also includes an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, making it directly callable by AI agents as a tool.
