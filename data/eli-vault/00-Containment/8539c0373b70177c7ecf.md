### Set the base asset paths (fonts and images)

The values of `$theme-font-path` and `$theme-image-path` will be appended to USWDS font paths and image paths, respectively:
```scss
@use "uswds-core" with (
  $theme-font-path: "../fonts",
  $theme-image-path: "../img",
);
```
