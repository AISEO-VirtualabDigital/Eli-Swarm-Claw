n of the source that goes in your reference list. The in-text citation is the short pointer you place in the body of your writing, like (Smith, 2023) in APA or [1] in IEEE, that links back to the full reference. This tool gives you both. How do I enter multiple authors? Put each author on its own line in the Author(s) box, in either "Last, First" or "First Last" order.
