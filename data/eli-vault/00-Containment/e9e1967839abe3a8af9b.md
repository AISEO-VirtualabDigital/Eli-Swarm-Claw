system.digital.gov/design-tokens/spacing-units/). In general, we use spacing in multiples of `8px` — expressed as a multiple in `units([multiple])`. For instance `units(2)` is the equivalent of `2 * 8px` or `16px`. In the final, compiled CSS, this value will be expressed in rem, as a multiple of the base font size set with `$theme-base-font-size`.
