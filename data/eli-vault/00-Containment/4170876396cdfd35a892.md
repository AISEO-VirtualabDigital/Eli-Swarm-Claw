# What Schema.org types does Claude SEO support?

JSON-LD is the preferred format (Google's stated preference). Claude SEO detects, validates, and generates the active Schema.org types documented in [skills/seo/references/schema-types.md](skills/seo/references/schema-types.md), including organization, article, product, local, event, job, course, software/application, service, Q&A, and video patterns.
