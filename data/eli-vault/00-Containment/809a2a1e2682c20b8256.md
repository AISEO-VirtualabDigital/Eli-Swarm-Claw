## BrowserClaw

**What is BrowserClaw?** BrowserClaw is a free, open-source browser your AI agents drive using your logged-in accounts. You install it like any browser, sign into the sites you use, and give tasks to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible AI.
