### Ahrefs Metrics (Paid)
For Ahrefs subscribers, the toolbar unlocks domain and page-level metrics directly on every site or search result:
- Keyword rankings and counts
- Traffic estimates
- Referring domains count
- Domain Rating (DR)
- URL Rating (UR)
## Pricing
- Core tools: **Free** (on-page audit, link highlight, redirect trace, user agent switch)
