### Can my AI agent manage my YouTube channel?

Yes — with Mode 2 (Hosted MCP) or Mode 3 (Local MCP), your agent connects to your channel via OAuth and can read your private analytics (watch time, retention, traffic sources) and write back SEO updates (title, description, tags) directly. Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Codex, Gemini CLI, or any MCP-compatible client.
