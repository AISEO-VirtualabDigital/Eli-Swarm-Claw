### Paid Platform Features (Reference Only)

These require a Surfer subscription but are worth knowing about for client recommendations:

- **Content Editor** — Write content with real-time SEO guidelines
- **AI Search Visibility Monitor** — Track brand in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity answers
- **Topic Ideas** — Discover content angles matching audience intent
- **Site Audit** — Complete SEO audit and plan

### Strategic Value for VirtuaLab

1. **AI Detection Checkpoint:** Before publishing any Eli-generated content, run it through Surfer's AI Detector to ensure it passes. This is especially important for parasite SEO where platforms may penalize AI content.

2. **Humanization Pipeline:** Eli generates draft → Surfer AI Humanizer processes it → Claude does final polish. Three-layer humanization.

3. **AI Search Visibility Monitoring:** Surfer's paid feature tracks brand mentions across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity — complementary to KE's AI Brand Tracker (which only tracks ChatGPT).
